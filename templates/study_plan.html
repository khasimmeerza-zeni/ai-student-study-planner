How the Study Plan Algorithm Works (For Faculty Presentation)
1
Exam Proximity (Urgency)

$U_i$ increases as days remaining decrease. If exam is $\le 3$ days away, urgency multiplier spikes to $3.0\times$ to maximize emergency focus.

2
Subject Difficulty

Hard subjects receive a $1.75\times$ coefficient, Medium subjects receive $1.25\times$, and Easy subjects receive $1.0\times$.

3
Preparation Deficit

Deficit is calculated as $\frac{100 - \text{prep\%}}{100} + 0.25$. Subjects with low preparation require proportionally higher study time.

4
Normalization & Distribution

Raw weights $W_i = \text{Diff} \times \text{Deficit} \times \text{Urgency}$ are normalized to allocate weekly hours and split into bite-sized 1-2.5 hour daily tasks.
